# Sunshine Construction Risk Register

## Project: Sunshine Residential Construction Project

A risk register is used to identify, analyze, and manage potential events that could impact project cost, schedule, quality, or scope.

---

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Category | Probability | Impact | Risk Level | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R001 | Material delivery delays | Supply Chain | Medium | High | High | Develop supplier backup plans and monitor deliveries |
| R002 | Budget overrun | Financial | Medium | High | High | Track costs regularly and perform budget reviews |
| R003 | Construction schedule delays | Schedule | Medium | High | High | Monitor progress and adjust resources when needed |
| R004 | Labor shortage | Resource | Low | Medium | Medium | Maintain qualified labor pool and workforce planning |
| R005 | Safety incidents | Safety | Low | High | High | Conduct safety training and regular inspections |
| R006 | Weather impact | External | Medium | Medium | Medium | Include schedule contingency time |

---

# Risk Management Process

## Risk Identification
- Review project activities
- Consult stakeholders
- Analyze historical issues

## Risk Assessment
- Evaluate probability
- Evaluate impact
- Assign risk priority

## Risk Response

Strategies include:

- Avoid
- Reduce
- Transfer
- Accept

---

## Risk Monitoring

Risks will be reviewed regularly through:

- Project meetings
- Progress reports
- Dashboard tracking
